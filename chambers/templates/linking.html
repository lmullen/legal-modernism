Match tiers

The tier records how far the linker got: which probe produced a link, or which step of the cascade ran out of things to match against. Percentages are of the status, not of the corpus. Citations turned away before any probe (skipped_junk, skipped_not_whitelisted) carry no tier and are not counted here.

Case names

The detector records the case name printed before each citation (case_name, #387) and how it found it: direct, printed before the citation; parallel, inherited from the preceding parallel cite; table, the plaintiff taken from the row above in a table of cases. How far the names reach, by status and by match tier. A real case citation almost always has a name before it and a statute rarely does, so the share is also a reading of what each status holds. Whether a name agrees with the case its citation linked to is judged live on the citation, page and case pages, not here.
