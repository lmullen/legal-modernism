Against CAP's citation graph

CAP publishes its own case-to-case citation graph (cap_citations.citations), drawn from the same cases by its own extractor. Here our links from one CAP case to another are set beside it, and the unit changes: above, a citation; here, an edge, a citing case and a cited case, which a case citing another three times makes once. The comparison covers the citing cases decided by 1920 that have an opinion and that CAP's graph includes{{with .Cases}} ({{num .Cases}} cases){{end}}, and only edges between two CAP cases: an opinion citing its own case, and our links to the English Reports, code reporters and stub cases, have no counterpart in CAP's graph and are left out. Neither graph is the truth. Recall is the share of CAP's edges we also have; precision the share of ours CAP also has.
